# Abu Al-Arabi Platform

# Study Room 2.0 - Premium Learning Workspace

You are a Senior Product Designer, Senior Full Stack Engineer, UX Architect, and Frontend Specialist.

Your task is NOT to slightly improve the current Study Room.

Your task is to completely redesign and rebuild it into a premium digital study workspace inspired by:

- GoodNotes

- Notability

- Apple Notes

- PDF Expert

while keeping the visual identity of Abu Al-Arabi Platform.

This must become the main place where students spend most of their study time.

------------------------------------------------------------

GENERAL GOAL

------------------------------------------------------------

The student should never need to download a PDF and open GoodNotes.

Everything should happen inside the website.

The Study Room should feel like a native iPad application.

------------------------------------------------------------

REMOVE CURRENT DESIGN

------------------------------------------------------------

The current page looks like a simple Pomodoro timer.

This is NOT acceptable.

Completely redesign the page.

------------------------------------------------------------

TOP TOOLBAR

LEFT SIDEBAR

CENTER PDF READER
